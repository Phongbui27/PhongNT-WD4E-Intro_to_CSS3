Famous Local Dishes

Da Nang is famous for its delicious traditional food. Here are some dishes you should try when visiting the city.

Mi Quang

A traditional noodle dish with flavorful broth, meat and herbs.

Banh Xeo

Crispy Vietnamese pancakes filled with shrimp, pork and bean sprouts.

Bun Cha Ca

A delicious noodle soup made with fish cakes and fresh vegetables.
